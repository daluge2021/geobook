## Start Here

Q: What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?
A: GEO is the practice of optimizing digital content so AI-powered search engines and large language models cite, reference, and recommend your brand in generated answers.
Source: https://geo010.com/fundamentals/what-is-geo.html

Q: How do GEO and SEO work together?
A: SEO optimizes for ranking in search results; GEO optimizes for being cited in AI answers. They compound — GEO builds on SEO fundamentals but focuses on answer assets, entity density, and brand-as-source.
Source: https://geo010.com/fundamentals/seo-vs-geo.html
