I. Fundamentals

Generative Engine Optimization · 7 Articles

What Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is, why AI answer engines change how brands earn visibility, and the trust, orientation and answer-asset basics every other chapter builds on.

🤖 Open to AI Crawlers · Welcome to Cite

What does this chapter cover?

This chapter defines Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and explains why AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude — change how brands earn visibility. It covers the shift from ranked link lists to cited answers, the E-E-A-T trust signals AI uses when choosing sources, the llms.txt orientation file, answer assetization, and a checklist to start from zero.

Read it first if you are new to the field: each later chapter assumes the vocabulary introduced here.

GEO does not replace SEO — it adds a layer that makes your content quotable by AI. Start here, then move to Metrics to learn how to measure the result.
