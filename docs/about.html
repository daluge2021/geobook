About GEO Encyclopedia — Mission, Editorial Standards & Methods

GEO Encyclopedia is an open knowledge base on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), maintained by a team of GEO researchers and practitioners.
Everything we publish exists for one purpose: to help brands understand how to be seen, cited, and recommended by AI in the generative search era.

1. What Is GEO Encyclopedia?

GEO Encyclopedia is a public, ad-supported knowledge resource covering the complete GEO knowledge system: 48 in-depth articles across 9 chapters, plus 10 glossary terms and a Community hub, from fundamentals to real-world implementation.

Our content is written in plain, action-oriented English. Each article is designed to be directly usable — whether you are a marketer planning a GEO roadmap, a founder assessing AI-era visibility, or an SEO professional extending your skill set to generative engines.

2. Our Mission

  • Make GEO a methodology, not a buzzword. We translate scattered research and vendor marketing into a repeatable, step-by-step system.
  • Explain how AI actually decides. From RAG retrieval to E-E-A-T, we ground every recommendation in how modern AI engines select, rank, and cite sources.
  • Keep the whole field readable. Our glossary and chapter structure let newcomers and experts move at their own pace.

3. Editorial Standards — Every Article Passes a Four-Dimension Check

Before publication, every piece of content is checked against four dimensions (E-E-A-T). These are the same standards we recommend to the brands we write for:

DimensionStandard
ExperienceBacked by first-hand cases, field observations, and hands-on records — not speculation.
ExpertiseGrounded in technical parameters, industry standards (e.g., ISO), and verifiable data.
AuthoritativenessReferences industry associations, universities, and independent research institutions.
TrustworthinessContact information is genuine, data is attributed to checkable sources, and claims can be verified.

In practice this means:

  • ✅ Every statistic we cite is linked to a verifiable public source (research paper, official documentation, or industry report).
  • ✅ Case-study articles explicitly state when they are illustrative composites — synthesized from real project methodologies to protect client confidentiality.
  • ✅ Each article carries its author (our Editorial Team) and its last-updated date.
  • ✅ We correct errors and update content as the field evolves; outdated claims are revised or removed.

4. How We Produce Content

  • Primary sources first. We rely on academic papers, official documentation from search engines and AI platforms, and named industry reports.
  • Cross-verification. Significant figures are checked against at least two independent sources when available.
  • Regular review. Articles are reviewed and re-dated when the underlying facts change.
  • Transparent corrections. If you spot an error, tell us — see Contact.

5. A Note on Advertisements

This site is free to read and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by third-party networks and are clearly separate from our editorial content. Advertising never influences our rankings, recommendations, or editorial decisions.

Questions, corrections, or collaboration ideas? Reach the editorial team at our Contact page — we read every message.
