What is GEO? — A Complete Guide to Generative Engine Optimization

Last updated: 2026-07-08 · By GEO Encyclopedia Editorial Team

In one sentence:

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a methodology for optimizing brand content to be prioritized in AI answers, targeting the retrieval and generation mechanisms of AI large language models (such as ChatGPT, Doubao, DeepSeek, and Gemini).

I. The Background of GEO's Emergence

The birth of GEO stems from an irreversible search revolution—zero-click search. When users search for questions on AI platforms like ChatGPT, Doubao, and DeepSeek, the AI generates answers directly, and users no longer need to click on any website links.

According to SparkToro research, approximately 68% of Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 — up from 60% in 2024. Gartner predicts that by 2026, traditional search engine traffic will decline by 25% due to AI chatbots and other virtual agents. This means the "search traffic" model that businesses have relied on in the past is being reshaped, and brands need an AI-oriented optimization methodology—this is GEO.

II. The Core Mechanism of GEO

The underlying technical foundation of GEO is RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). When users ask questions on AI platforms, the system executes three steps:

  1. Retrieval—Converts the question into semantic vectors and searches the internet for the most relevant document fragments
  2. Augmented—Attaches the retrieved content as reference material to the question
  3. Generation—AI generates natural language answers based on "question + reference material"

The core of GEO is to make your content selected by the AI during the "retrieval phase." The AI's criteria for selecting whom to cite have three dimensions: relevance (content directly answers the question), credibility (meets E-E-A-T standards), and structural clarity (well-maintained Schema markup).

GEO is not a replacement for SEO, but an evolved version. A good SEO foundation is a prerequisite for GEO success, and both can be optimized in parallel.

IV. The Four Steps of GEO

  1. Diagnose the Current State—Search for brand keywords on AI platforms to understand the current citation situation
  2. Answer Asset Creation—Write frequently asked customer questions into standard answers that provide immediate answers at the beginning
  3. Technical Implementation—Add Schema structured data, configure LLMs.txt, and open up AI crawlers
  4. Monitor and Iterate—Regularly measure citation share and optimize content

V. Why Do GEO Now?

  • 90% of businesses haven't entered the game yet—Now is the window period for low-cost acquisition of AI mindshare
  • Customer acquisition costs reduced by 32%-62%—AI recommendations come with built-in trust, leading to higher conversion rates
  • Visible results in 1-3 months—AI models update frequently, and the speed of results is much faster than SEO
