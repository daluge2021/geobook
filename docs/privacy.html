Short version: geo010.com is an ad-supported, free-to-read knowledge site. We use Google AdSense to show ads and an anonymous visitor counter. We do not sell your data, and we do not collect account or profile information.

2. Advertising and Google Cookies

This site runs advertising served by Google AdSense. When ads are displayed, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. These cookies help Google decide how often you see an ad, and support both personalized and non-personalized advertising.

DoubleClick / ad cookies

Google's advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) can be used to build an interest profile for personalized ads. If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you have two easy options:

  • Use the Ads Settings page: google.com/settings/ads
  • Visit aboutads.info/choices (Digital Advertising Alliance) to opt out of third-party vendors

You can also read Google's full explanation of how advertising uses cookies at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What this means in practice

We never see the contents of Google's advertising cookies. Google and other advertising networks may use them to tailor the ads shown on this and other websites. Opting out stops personalization, but ads may still appear based on the content of the page.
