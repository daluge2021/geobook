What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing your brand's visibility within AI-generated answers. As search engines like Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and DeepSeek increasingly provide direct AI answers instead of blue link lists, businesses must ensure their content is cited, referenced, and recommended by these AI systems.

This encyclopedia covers the complete GEO knowledge system — from understanding zero-click search trends and E-E-A-T trust signals, to implementing Schema structured data, managing AI crawler access, and measuring citation share.
