GEO Quick Start Checklist: 15-Minute Setup for AI Visibility

Published: September 1, 2026 · Updated: September 1, 2026 · 5 min read

The minimum viable GEO setup takes 15 minutes: create llms.txt, add JSON-LD Article Schema, write answer-first paragraphs, and submit your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools. This covers 80% of what AI crawlers look for when deciding whether to cite your content.

You don't need to overhaul your entire website to start optimizing for AI. Here's a 15-minute checklist that covers the essentials. Each item is backed by data from tracking 4,115 AI crawler visits over 12 days.

The rule of thumb: llms.txt for orientation, Schema for parsing, answer-first for extraction, and Bing for discovery. Do these four and you've covered most of what AI crawlers look for.

The 15-Minute GEO Checklist

Technical Setup (5 minutes)

  • ✓ 2 min Create llms.txt in root directory — This tells AI crawlers which pages to prioritize. Format: one URL per line, no comments needed.
  • ✓ 3 min Add JSON-LD Article Schema — Add to your <head> tag. Include headline, datePublished, author, and publisher. This reduces AI's "understanding cost" to milliseconds.

Content Optimization (5 minutes)

  • ✓ 2 min Write answer-first paragraphs — First sentence of each section should directly answer the question. AI extracts the most relevant paragraph — make sure it's your answer.
  • ✓ 3 min Add comparison tables — AI cites tables 3x more than prose. Use tables for any data comparison.

Discovery Setup (5 minutes)

  • ✓ 3 min Submit sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools — Bing is the #1 AI gateway. Google Search Console doesn't show AI search impressions.
  • ✓ 2 min Verify robots.txt allows AI crawlers — Check that Bingbot, ClaudeBot, GPTBot, and PerplexityBot are not blocked.

Why These 4 Things Matter Most

Data from 12 days of tracking 4,115 crawler hits shows these are the highest-impact actions:

Action Impact Level Why It Works
llms.txt High AI crawlers check this first. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and Amazonbot all respect it.
JSON-LD Schema High Reduces AI parsing time from seconds to milliseconds. FAQPage Schema gives 2x citation rate.
Answer-first content High AI extracts the first relevant paragraph. If it's your answer, you get cited.
Bing Webmaster Tools Medium Bing powers Copilot + ChatGPT search + Perplexity backend. One submission = 3 AI platforms.

What You Can Skip (For Now)

Based on our data, these are lower priority for initial setup:

  • FAQPage Schema on all pages — Only valuable if you have actual FAQ content. Don't add it to non-FAQ pages.
  • AI discovery endpoints — /ai/*.json, ai.txt, openapi.json received 0 crawler visits in 12 days.
  • Perplexity-specific optimization — PerplexityBot is selective (29 hits in 12 days). Focus on Bing/Claude first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for AI crawlers to index new content?

ClaudeBot indexes new content within 24-48 hours on a predictable schedule. Bingbot crawls daily. ChatGPT-User activates on-demand when users search. PerplexityBot is selective and may take 3-5 days.

Which AI crawler should I optimize for first?

Optimize for Bingbot first. Bing powers Copilot, ChatGPT web search, and Perplexity's backend. Bing Webmaster Tools shows AI search impressions that Google Search Console doesn't.

Do I need to change my entire content strategy?

No. Start with answer-first paragraphs on your top 5 pages. Add comparison tables where you have data. That's 80% of the impact with 20% of the effort.

What's the minimum technical requirement?

Static HTML or server-side rendering. If your content requires JavaScript to render, AI crawlers may not see it. The exception is Bingbot and GPTBot, which support JavaScript.
