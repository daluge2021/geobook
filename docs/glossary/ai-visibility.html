What is AI Visibility Index? — Measuring GEO Performance

Last updated: 2026-07-16 · By GEO Encyclopedia Editorial Team · Back to Chapter 1

One-Line Definition:

The AI Visibility Index is a comprehensive metric that measures how often a brand is mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers — reflecting "when AI discusses topics in your industry, how likely is your name to appear in the AI's response."

1. Why Are New Metrics Needed?

Traditional SEO metrics — "what rank are you" — don't work well in GEO because:

  • AI answers have randomness (asking the same question twice may yield different answers)
  • AI doesn't show "1st place, 2nd place" — it generates a block of text
  • Different AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.) have different preferences

So the AI Visibility Index is a probability-based composite score, typically presented as 0–100.

2. Measurement Method

Most GEO monitoring tools (Profound, SEMrush, BrightEdge, etc.) work this way:

  1. Select a topic set — Your core keywords and questions (about 20–50)
  2. Multi-platform querying — Repeated queries across multiple AI platforms (typically 5–8)
  3. Content analysis — Using NLP to analyze AI answers, extracting brand mentions, link citations, contextual sentiment, and position
  4. Composite scoring — Calculating a comprehensive index based on the above data

3. Three Key Sub-Metrics

MetricMeaningSource
Brand Mention RateHow often the brand is mentioned in AI answers about relevant topicsBasic metric
Citation ShareYour content's share among all sources cited by AIGEO monitoring tools & manual testing
Description AccuracyWhether AI's description of your brand is correctContent quality metric

4. How to Set a Baseline?

It's recommended to conduct a baseline measurement on the very first day of starting GEO optimization:

  1. List 10 topics you most want to appear in
  2. Query on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI platforms
  3. Record: Were you mentioned? How were you described? How many competitors were mentioned?
  4. Re-measure monthly to observe trends
